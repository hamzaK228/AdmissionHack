# Research Brief — CSCA (China Scholastic Competency Assessment)

> Researched September 2026. The newest mandatory exam on the global admission landscape —
> a rare greenfield opportunity because no established prep ecosystem exists yet.

## CSCA (来华留学本科入学学业水平测试)

- **Full name + body:** China Scholastic Competency Assessment, organized by the **China
  Scholarship Council (CSC)** with university experts.
- **Purpose:** Standardized entrance exam for **non-Chinese citizens applying to undergraduate
  programs in China** — evaluates language proficiency and academic readiness, and feeds both
  admission and scholarship decisions (including the Chinese Government Scholarship).
  Chinese passport holders and HK/Macau/Taiwan residence permit holders cannot register.
- **Mandatory status:** Policy update for 2026 admissions — required by most Chinese universities
  for international undergraduate applicants from the **2026–2027 academic years**; mandatory
  subjects for Government Scholarship applicants are **Math + Professional Chinese** (STEM or
  Humanities track). Professional Chinese is mandatory for Chinese-taught programs.
- **Subjects & format** (all single-answer multiple choice):

| Subject | Language | Duration | Questions | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Humanities Chinese | Chinese only | 90 min | 80 MC | 0–100 |
| STEM Chinese | Chinese only | 90 min | 80 MC | 0–100 |
| Mathematics | Chinese or English | 60 min | 48 MC | 0–100 |
| Physics | Chinese or English | 60 min | 48 MC | 0–100 |
| Chemistry | Chinese or English | 60 min | 48 MC | 0–100 |

  Physics/Chemistry optional unless the target university requires them. Universities verify scores
  and see candidates' global percentile ranking via the official system.
- **Cost:** 450 CNY (~$63) for one subject; 700 CNY (~$98) for two or more (VISA/MasterCard/UnionPay).
- **Frequency:** **5 sessions/year from 2026** (January, March, April, June, December) with time
  zones rotated to favor different regions. First global session: December 21, 2025.
- **Delivery:** Initially **remote home testing** with dual-device proctoring (64-bit Windows
  required, ≥20 Mbps symmetric bandwidth); onsite test centers launched first in Vietnam and
  Thailand in 2025, more countries planned. Home testing is banned where a local center exists.
- **Score validity:** **2 years** from issuance; results within 7 working days, electronic reports
  submitted directly to institutions.
- **Registration:** via [csca.cn](https://csca.cn); no age limit (under-14 needs guardian consent);
  no limit on attempts or subjects per session; official syllabi (2025 editions) at csca.cn/testprep.
- **Popularity:** New — no volume data yet. Context: China hosts ~500k international students and
  the exam becomes the mandatory gate for undergrad admissions from 2026–27.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Math module drills (sets/inequalities, functions, geometry/algebra,
  probability & statistics), Physics and Chemistry module drills per official syllabus, **Chinese
  language modules** (character recognition, vocabulary, sentence completion, reading — the unique
  content), technical setup guide for remote proctoring, mock tests mirroring the 0–100 MC format,
  university/scholarship score-requirement database, subject-combination guidance per major.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** **5 for the China corridor** (every China-bound
  undergrad needs it from 2026–27).

## Why this matters to AdmissionHack

1. **Greenfield SEO:** the exam is brand new — search competition is near zero, and the mandatory
   policy guarantees demand growth through 2027+. First movers will own the SERP.
2. **Template reuse is high:** Math/Physics/Chemistry content overlaps with our SAT/ACT and
   A-Level prep; only the Chinese-language modules are unique content.
3. **Corridor size:** the China-bound corridor (esp. Belt & Road scholarship students from Asia,
  Africa, CIS) is one of the largest international flows without a dedicated prep platform yet.
4. **Suggested placement:** treat as **Tier 1.5 — "new-opportunity bet"**; ideally ship alongside
   or immediately after the Tier 1 English hubs (IELTS/TOEFL/DET), because CSCA + English test
   prep is the complete China-corridor bundle.
