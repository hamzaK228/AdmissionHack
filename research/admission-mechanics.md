# Admission Mechanics by Destination — verified September 2026

> Primary source for country-guide data on the site. All figures 2025–2026 cycle; "approx." = varies by consulate/university, re-indexed annually.

## United States
- **Windows**: Common App opens Aug 1 · ED/EA ~Nov 1 · Regular Jan 1–15 · rolling Jan–Mar. Fall main; some Spring (Jan).
- **Fees**: $50–90 per university (avg ~$75); waivers common; Common App platform free.
- **English**: IELTS 6.5–7.0 / TOEFL 80–100 / DET 110–125 / PTE 58–65.
- **Docs**: transcripts, essays, 2–3 LORs, English test; SAT/ACT if required; financial docs only at I-20 stage.
- **Tests/interviews**: many elites reinstated SAT/ACT (MIT, Harvard, Yale, Princeton, Stanford, Caltech, Cornell…); alumni interviews optional.
- **Visa**: F-1 · financial proof = full first-year cost on I-20 (~$45k–90k) · SEVIS $350 + MRV $185 · processing 2–8+ weeks · post-study **OPT 12 mo + 24-mo STEM extension**.
- **Scholarships**: Fulbright (deadlines Feb–Oct by country); need-blind full aid at Harvard/Yale/Princeton/MIT/Amherst; institutional merit.
- **Gotchas**: test-optional is over — check each school; ED is binding; I-20 proof must cover tuition AND living.

## United Kingdom
- **Windows**: UCAS opens early Sept · equal consideration ~14 Jan · Oxbridge + medicine 15 Oct. Fall intake.
- **Fees**: UCAS £28.95 one fee for up to 5 choices (2026 entry; £34.50 for 2027); no UG university fees.
- **English**: IELTS 6.0–6.5 (band minima 5.5–6.0) / TOEFL 79–92 / PTE 59–62; SELT usually enough.
- **Docs**: one personal statement for all 5 choices, reference, predicted grades; ATAS for sensitive STEM.
- **Tests/interviews**: Oxbridge interviews Nov–Dec + admissions tests (TSA/ESAT/TMUA/LNAT); UCAT for medicine (register ~May).
- **Visa**: Student visa · **£1,529/mo London, £1,171/mo outside (9 mo, held 28 consecutive days)** + unpaid tuition · ~3 weeks · post-study **Graduate Route 2 yrs (3 PhD)**.
- **Scholarships**: Chevening (Aug–early Nov); Commonwealth Master's (Sep–Oct); GREAT Scholarships (Apr–Jun).
- **Gotchas**: never book a visa without CAS; 5-choice limit + single personal statement; maintenance funds must sit 28 days untouched.

## Canada
- **Windows**: open late Sept/Oct · Ontario equal consideration mid-Jan · UBC/McGill ~Jan 15 · most close Jan–Mar. Fall main; some Winter.
- **Fees**: CA$75–150 per university; OUAC CA$156 for up to 3 choices + CA$50 each extra; supplemental fees CA$75–150.
- **English**: IELTS 6.5 / TOEFL 86–100 / DET 115–125; Québec francophone TFI/TEF.
- **Docs**: transcripts, predicted grades; UBC Personal Profile, Waterloo AIF; **CAQ** for Québec after acceptance.
- **Tests/interviews**: rare for UG; supplemental apps at competitive programs.
- **Visa**: Study permit · **CA$23,448/yr living (Sept 2026) + tuition; GIC ~CA$25k standard · PAL (Provincial Attestation Letter) mandatory** · 4–12 weeks · post-study **PGWP up to 3 yrs** (bachelor+ exempt from field limits).
- **Scholarships**: Lester B. Pearson (U of T; nominate by Oct, apply Jan); UBC International Scholars (Nov–Dec); Vanier CGS (research).
- **Gotchas**: SDS fast-track abolished (Nov 2024); PAL before applying; PGWP eligibility now depends on program/field.

## Germany
- **Windows**: Winter: 15 Jan (NC/hochschulstart) or 15 Jul (uni-assist, varies) · Summer: 15 Jan · English master's Oct–Mar.
- **Fees**: uni-assist €75 first + €30 each extra per semester; direct often free.
- **English**: English-taught IELTS 6.0–6.5 / TOEFL 80–90; German-taught **DSH-2 or TestDaF TDN 4×4**.
- **Docs**: certified copies, translations; **APS certificate mandatory for China, India, Vietnam (allow 2–3 months)**; some need uni-assist VPD.
- **Tests**: none typically; numerus clausus grade-based; TMS for medicine; portfolios for arts.
- **Visa**: National D · **blocked account €11,904/yr (€992/mo)** · 6–12 weeks (appointment waits) · post-study **18-month job-seeking permit**.
- **Scholarships**: DAAD (often Oct–Dec deadlines); Deutschlandstipendium €300/mo; Erasmus Mundus (Oct–Jan).
- **Gotchas**: start APS months early; uni-assist takes 4–6 weeks, fees per semester; tuition-free = public unis only (Baden-Württemberg €1,500/sem non-EU).

## Netherlands
- **Windows**: Studielink opens ~1 Oct · **numerus fixus hard deadline 15 Jan** · other bachelor's ~1 May · master's Nov–May.
- **Fees**: Studielink free; non-EU processing €50–100 per university.
- **English**: IELTS 6.0–6.5 / TOEFL 80–90.
- **Docs**: diploma + transcript (Nuffic eval by university), motivation, CV; no apostille usually.
- **Tests**: selection only for numerus fixus (medicine, psychology, some tech) — 15 Jan + tests/interviews.
- **Visa**: MVV + residence permit **filed by the university** · proof ~€13,600–13,800/yr + tuition · 2–4 weeks · post-study **Orientation Year (zoekjaar) 12 mo**.
- **Scholarships**: NL Scholarship €5,000 (Feb–May); Orange Tulip (Feb–May); university excellence awards.
- **Gotchas**: 15 Jan numerus-fixus is absolute; Studielink ≠ application (finish the university portal too); housing crisis — arrange early.

## Australia
- **Windows**: Sem 1 (Feb): apply Jun–Dec · Sem 2 (Jul): Feb–May · rolling via agents/direct.
- **Fees**: A$0–125 (often free via agents).
- **English**: IELTS 6.5 (6.0 bands) / PTE 58–65; **new-format TOEFL not accepted for visas — take IELTS/PTE**.
- **Docs**: certified transcripts; **Genuine Student (GS) statements** central to visa (replaced GTE Mar 2024).
- **Tests**: UCAT ANZ + MMI for medicine; else grades-based.
- **Visa**: subclass 500 · **A$29,710/yr living + tuition · visa charge A$2,500 (from 1 Jul 2026)** · 4–16 weeks · post-study **485: 2 yrs bachelor / 3 yrs master's** (age cap 35).
- **Scholarships**: Australia Awards (1 Feb–30 Apr); Destination Australia; RTP stipends (research, Aug–Oct).
- **Gotchas**: world's most expensive student visa + rising refusals; intake caps can close early; TOEFL format trap.

## Italy
- **Windows**: university windows Feb–Jul · **Universitaly pre-enrollment by ~end July** · IMAT mid-Sept.
- **Fees**: Universitaly free; university fees €30–100; state tuition €156–4,000 income-based.
- **English**: IELTS 6.0+ (some 5.5–6.5) or university's own test.
- **Docs**: translated/legalized diploma; **Declaration of Value (DoV) or CIMEA statements (1–3 months — start early)**; pre-enrollment; financial + accommodation proof.
- **Tests**: IMAT (English medicine, Sept); TOLC/CISIA for many programs.
- **Visa**: Type D · proof ~€460/mo (~€6,000/yr, consulate-dependent) · 4–8 weeks · post-study **12-month job-seeking permit**.
- **Scholarships**: MAECI (Mar–May); Invest Your Talent (Feb–Mar); DSU regional grants + waivers (Jun–Sept).
- **Gotchas**: late Universitaly = no visa even with admission; DoV vs CIMEA confusion; DSU deadlines come before arrival.

## Türkiye
- **Windows**: TR-YÖS apply 14 Jan–3 Feb, exams ~Mar & Oct · preferences Jun–Aug · private unis rolling Jan–Aug · **Türkiye Bursları 10 Jan–20 Feb**.
- **Fees**: TR-YÖS ~$40–60; university apps mostly free (private some $50–100).
- **English**: IELTS 6.0–6.5 / TOEFL ~79 / university internal exam; Turkish-taught **TÖMER B2–C1** or prep year.
- **Docs**: notarized/translated diploma + transcript, passport, YÖS/SAT/IB scores — **each university publishes its accepted-exam list**.
- **Tests**: TR-YÖS; private unis accept SAT (minima ~1200+); Bursları has interview stage.
- **Visa**: student visa then **residence permit within 30 days of arrival** · no fixed national amount (~$1,000–2,000/sponsor) · 2–6 weeks · post-study 1-year job-search permit.
- **Scholarships**: Türkiye Bursları (fully funded, ~6,000 awards, Jan–Feb); Koç/Bilkent/Sabancı full awards.
- **Gotchas**: YÖS scores may not transfer between universities; 30-day residence window; Turkish-taught = +1 prep year.

## Japan
- **Windows**: April intake: apply Sept–Feb (EJU June & Nov) · SGU English programs ~Nov–Mar · **MEXT embassy track Apr–Jun** (year before).
- **Fees**: EJU ¥10,000–19,000; university exam fees ¥18,000–35,000.
- **English**: SGU TOEFL 79–90 / IELTS 6.0–6.5; Japanese-track **EJU Japanese + JLPT N2–N1** (EJU usually required, not JLPT alone).
- **Docs**: EJU/JLPT, translated transcripts, essays, LORs (SGU); **CoE** documents post-admission (sponsor bank statement ~¥1.2–2.5M).
- **Tests**: university-specific exams + interviews (often online for internationals).
- **Visa**: via **CoE (university files; 1–3 months)** + stamp 1–2 weeks · proof ~¥1.5–2M/yr · post-study **Designated Activities job-hunting 6 mo (renewable once)**.
- **Scholarships**: MEXT (embassy Apr–Jun); JASSO Honors ~¥48,000/mo; university reductions 30–100%.
- **Gotchas**: full timeline runs 12+ months before April entry; EJU only in select overseas cities; no centralized application — per-university exams.

## China
- **Windows**: Fall: apply Sept–Mar/Apr (many close 31 Mar) · Spring: Sept–Dec · **CSC Type A early–mid Feb, Type B Jan–Mar**.
- **Fees**: **$50–150 (CNY 400–900) per university**; CSC free but university fees often still apply.
- **English**: IELTS 6.0 / TOEFL 80 (some accept high-school English or interview); Chinese-taught **HSK 4–5** (HSK 5 realistic for bachelor's).
- **Docs**: notarized transcripts, **physical examination form + non-criminal-record certificate (notarized, fresh)**, passport, personal statement; JW202 from university.
- **Tests/interviews**: online interviews common; CSCA aptitude test newly required for some missions.
- **Visa**: X1 (convert to residence permit within 30 days) / X2 · proof varies (~$2,500–10,000) · 2–4 weeks · post-study: no general work visa (city pilots only).
- **Scholarships**: CSC (Dec–Apr); Schwarzman (Tsinghua, ~Sept); provincial scholarships.
- **Gotchas**: HSK 5 realistic floor; medical + non-criminal certificates cause most delays; CSC deadlines far earlier than self-funded rounds.

## Singapore
- **Windows**: NUS international: **3 Dec–23 Feb** (AY26/27) · NTU ~Oct–Feb/Mar. One main intake (Aug).
- **Fees**: NUS S$20; NTU ~S$30–100.
- **English**: IELTS 6.5–7.0 / TOEFL 92–100 (NUS/NTU benchmarks); DET ~120 at some.
- **Docs**: transcripts + forecast results, English test, CCA records; **MOE Tuition Grant** acceptance (3-year bond) at enrollment.
- **Tests/interviews**: interviews for competitive programs (medicine, scholarships).
- **Visa**: Student's Pass via **SOLAR+** (IPA letter) · no fixed funds (ICA assesses) · 2–4 weeks · post-study: **no automatic permit — Employment Pass needs S$5,600+ (S$6,200 finance)**.
- **Scholarships**: ASEAN UG Scholarship (Oct–Feb); Science & Technology UG Scholarship; **MOE Tuition Grant** (huge subsidy + 3-year bond).
- **Gotchas**: the Tuition Grant bond is a legal work obligation; Feb–Mar deadlines are hard; no open post-study period.

## South Korea
- **Windows**: March intake: apply **Sept–Nov** · September intake: Apr–Jun · GKS Feb–Mar (grad) / Sep (undergrad embassy).
- **Fees**: ₩60,000–150,000 (~$50–120) per university; multiple applications normal.
- **English**: English-track IELTS 5.5–6.5 / TOEFL 71–80; Korean-track **TOPIK 3–4+** (TOPIK 4 typical for Seoul; 3+ = GKS bonus).
- **Docs**: transcripts + diploma with **apostille or consular confirmation (Korea-specific)**, notarized translations, study plan, LORs, financial proof.
- **Tests/interviews**: interviews common (online); document screening dominant for UG.
- **Visa**: D-2 · **bank balance ~US$20,000 (₩20M) for greater-Seoul universities (some lowered to ~$15,400 in 2026)** · GKS exempt · 2–8 weeks · post-study **D-10 job-seeking up to 2 yrs**, then E-7/F-2-7 points.
- **Scholarships**: GKS (Feb–Mar / Sep); university scholarships 30–100% for TOPIK 5–6 / high GPA.
- **Gotchas**: apostilled documents take 4–8 weeks; TOPIK runs ~5×/year — miss a sitting, lose an intake; spring-intake deadlines land Sept–Nov of the previous year.

## United Arab Emirates
- **Windows**: rolling · Sept intake: apply Jan–Jun · federal universities (Khalifa, UAEU) fixed rounds ~Dec–Apr/May · Jan intake: Sept–Nov.
- **Fees**: AED 100–500 (~$30–140) per university.
- **English**: IELTS 6.0–6.5 / TOEFL 79+ / **EmSAT English 1100–1400 (valid 18 months)**; DET/PTE at some.
- **Docs**: **attested certificates + UAE MOE Equivalency Certificate** (chain: home MOE → UAE embassy → UAE MOFA/MOE) — slow, start immediately.
- **Tests/interviews**: some placement tests/interviews (Khalifa, UAEU engineering); EmSAT onshore.
- **Visa**: university-sponsored student residence visa · medical test + Emirates ID on arrival · 2–4 weeks · post-study: **no automatic permit — job-seeking entry permit 60–90 days; Golden Visa for top graduates**.
- **Scholarships**: Khalifa full scholarships (apply ~Dec–May); UAEU merit awards.
- **Gotchas**: equivalency chain blocks enrollment; EmSAT validity traps; no general post-study period.

## Typical English minimums (rule of thumb)
IELTS 6.5 overall / TOEFL 90 / DET 120 / PTE 60–65 at reputable universities across US, UK, CA, AU, DE, NL.
