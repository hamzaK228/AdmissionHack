## Key strategic findings

1. **SAT is the right first bet.** ~1.9–2.0M test-takers per class, relevance 5/5 for US undergrad,
   and demand is *rising* as test-optional reverses (MIT, Harvard, Yale, Stanford, Caltech
   reinstated testing 2024–26). Our SAT site lands on a growing market.
2. **English tests are the widest funnel** — every international applicant needs one, not just
   US-bound students. IELTS is the anchor (>4M tests/yr, volume leader, visa + admissions moat);
   TOEFL holds the US/Canada segment (~1.7M/yr).
3. **Growth bets: Duolingo DET and PTE.** DET bookings +40% YoY, $65, at-home, 5,500+ accepting
   institutions. PTE grew 822k → 1.23M tests in one year (+50%). Both are mainstream at
   university level now; IELTS keeps the visa/immigration moat.
4. **Recurring-revenue angle:** English test scores expire after **2 years** (IELTS/TOEFL/DET/PTE)
   and IELTS has One Skill Retake — users come back. GRE/GMAT scores last 5 years.
5. **ACT opportunity:** the Enhanced ACT (Sept 2025) made Science optional and changed the format —
   all legacy prep content is now stale. A new-format ACT hub is a content-gap play (1.37M takers).
6. **Avoid discontinued exams** — see the "do not build" list below.
7. **UK tests are consolidating:** Oxford announced (Jan 2026) it will replace its bespoke tests
   (MAT, PAT, TSA) with UAT-UK standardized tests from the 2027 entry cycle — build for the UAT-UK
   ecosystem (ESAT/TMUA), not per-Oxford-test sites.
8. **CSCA is the rarest kind of opportunity:** a mandatory exam created in 2025–26 (China-bound
   international undergrads, 5 sessions/year, run by the China Scholarship Council) with no
   established prep ecosystem to compete against. Math/Physics/Chemistry content reuses our SAT
   templates; only the Chinese-language modules are unique ([brief](exams-csca.md)).
9. **One hub template** serves all exams: format guide, section-by-section strategy, question banks
   by difficulty, mock tests, score calculator/concordance tables, requirement database
   ("which universities accept X score"), vocabulary/drill trainers where relevant.

## Do NOT build hubs for (discontinued)

- **SAT Subject Tests** (discontinued 2021) and **SAT Essay** (2021) — redirect to AP content
- **Paper SAT** — Digital SAT replaced it (intl. March 2023, US March 2024)
- **BMAT** — final sitting Oct 2023; all UK users moved to UCAT
- **NSAA / ENGAA** — replaced by ESAT (Oct 2024); keep only as redirect content
- **Old GMAT** (10th edition, 200–800) — retired Feb 2024; only Focus Edition (205–805) exists
- **Old long GRE** (pre-Sept 2023) — shortened format replaced it; label legacy content
- **Pre-2025 ACT** — Enhanced ACT replaced it; never teach mandatory-Science composite
- **UKCAT** — renamed UCAT in 2019; capture the keyword, don't build a separate hub
- **TOEFL Essentials** — still offered but niche (~500 programs); bundle into TOEFL content, no
  standalone hub
