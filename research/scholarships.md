## Advice patterns
1. **Timing**: big government windows close 8–14 months before enrollment — build a reverse calendar from your intake date.
2. **Nationality filters are hard filters**: Commonwealth, Australia Awards, DAAD EPOS (DAC list), OTS (NESO countries), Fulbright (commission in your country) — check before investing effort.
3. **Admission-first vs scholarship-first**: DAAD EPOS, SISGP, Commonwealth, Gates Cambridge, GREAT need university admission first/simultaneously; MEXT embassy, Türkiye Bursları, GKS embassy, CSC Type A secure placement through the scholarship itself. Confusing these voids applications.
4. **Interviews decide close calls**: Chevening, Fulbright, GKS, MEXT, Australia Awards score a concrete "impact back home" narrative — committees explicitly score return-home plans.
5. **Common disqualifiers**: host-country citizenship/PR (Australia Awards), missing work-experience minimums (Chevening 2,800 hrs; SISGP 3,000 hrs; EPOS 2 yrs), age caps (Eiffel <25, Türkiye <21/30/35, GKS <25/40), applying directly where nomination is required (Eiffel, Pearson, UBC).
