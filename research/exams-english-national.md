# Research Brief — English Proficiency Tests & National Entrance Exams (September 2026)

Figures marked "approx." are best-available estimates.

## SECTION A — ENGLISH PROFICIENCY TESTS

## IELTS (Academic)
- **Full name + administering body:** International English Language Testing System; jointly owned by British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia, and Cambridge Assessment English.
- **Purpose:** The default English gate for study abroad (UK, Australia, Canada, NZ, much of Europe), and for UK/Australian/NZ visa categories. Accepted by 12,000+ organizations in 140+ countries; also used for migration.
- **Format:** 4 sections — Listening (30 min), Reading (60 min), Writing (60 min), Speaking (11–14 min, face-to-face or video). Total ~2h45m. Band scale 0–9 in 0.5 steps. Paper-based or computer-delivered at test centers; IELTS Online available in some markets. IELTS One Skill Retake (retake a single section) available in many countries since 2023.
- **Cost:** approx. $200–$300 depending on country: India ₹18,000 (~$210, rising to ₹19,000 Apr 2026); US ~$280–350; UK ~£200–230.
- **Frequency:** Paper-based ~48 fixed dates/year; computer-delivered available near-daily in major cities; usually bookable 4+ times/month.
- **Score validity:** 2 years (institutions/immigration authorities enforce this strictly, though the certificate itself technically doesn't expire).
- **Popularity:** >4 million tests in 2023 (record, per ielts.org, Jan 2024) — the largest English test globally.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Skill-by-skill lessons (esp. Writing Task 1/2 essay templates and graph description), band descriptors, timed mock tests, band-score calculator, writing evaluation (AI or human), speaking practice with model answers, topic vocabulary banks, question-type drills (True/False/Not Given, matching headings, etc.).
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 5

## TOEFL iBT
- **Full name + administering body:** Test of English as a Foreign Language, Internet-Based Test; ETS (USA).
- **Purpose:** Most entrenched in US/Canada admissions; accepted by 13,000+ institutions worldwide. Historically the Americas/Asia workhorse; less weight in UK visa (not a SELT).
- **Format:** Shortened in 2023 to under 2 hours: Reading (~35 min), Listening (~36 min), Speaking (16 min, 4 tasks), Writing (~29 min, incl. "Academic Discussion" task); 10-min break removed; scale 0–120. Center-based or at-home edition; 2025–2026 updates introduced adaptive/multistage Reading & Listening (secondary sources).
- **Cost:** ~$180–$270 by country: India ~₹16,900–18,000 (~$180–195); US ~$185–200; range ~$157 (Turkey) to $450 (Switzerland).
- **Frequency:** 100+ scheduled dates/year globally; home edition available on more days.
- **Score validity:** 2 years.
- **Popularity:** approx. 1.7M/year (secondary-source estimate; ETS no longer publishes exact annual figures; >25M cumulative since launch).
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Academic-lecture listening practice, note-taking frameworks, speaking-response templates with self-recording tools, integrated writing drills, full-length adaptive mocks, score-conversion tables, vocabulary for academic contexts.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 5

## Duolingo English Test
- **Full name + administering body:** Duolingo English Test (DET); Duolingo, Inc. (USA).
- **Purpose:** Fast, cheap, at-home alternative to IELTS/TOEFL for university admissions; accepted by 5,500–6,500+ institutions (incl. ~98 of the top 100 US universities per Duolingo marketing; verify per program). Not accepted for UK student visas (not a SELT) and limited for visa routes.
- **Format:** ~1 hour, adaptive, fully online at home with AI + human proctoring; integrated skill tasks; overall score 10–160 plus Literacy/Comprehension/Conversation/Production subscores; includes video interview/writing sample. Results in ~48 hours.
- **Cost:** ~$65 single test / ~$110 for two-test bundle (US pricing; some sources cite up to $70 in 2026); unlimited score sends included — the cheapest major option.
- **Frequency:** On demand, 365 days/year (take within 21 days of purchase).
- **Score validity:** 2 years.
- **Popularity:** 3M+ cumulative test takers as of Sept 2025; DET bookings grew ~40%+ YoY in 2024 (Duolingo investor communications). Largest volume is among US-bound applicants and in China/India.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Question-type familiarity (C-test, listen-and-type, interactive reading/writing), vocabulary trainer, free official practice tests, score-to-IELTS/TOEFL concordance tables, admissions requirement database (score needed per university).
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 5

## PTE Academic
- **Full name + administering body:** Pearson Test of English Academic; Pearson (UK).
- **Purpose:** Fully AI-scored alternative to IELTS/TOEFL; strong in Australia/NZ, growing in UK/Canada/US. Accepted by 3,500+ institutions; approved SELT for UK visas (PTE Academic UKVI) and accepted in Australian migration; PTE Core added for Canadian economic immigration (2024).
- **Format:** ~2 hours, computer-based, 20 task types with integrated scoring (speaking into microphone, summarize written text etc.); scale 10–90. Center-based; PTE Academic Online (home) available for university use (not visas). Results typically within 1–2 days.
- **Cost:** approx. $185–$340 by country: India ₹15,900–17,000 (~$190–200); US ~$220; some markets ~$300–340.
- **Frequency:** On demand, year-round in 400+ cities (near-daily computer sessions in big markets).
- **Score validity:** 2 years for admissions/immigration use (some Australian skilled-migration processing uses a longer lookback window — verify per route).
- **Popularity:** 1.23M tests in 2023 (first year above 1M; up from 822k in 2022, ~50% growth — Pearson via StudyTravel News); Pearson claims ~3M tests/year across all its English products.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Task-type mastery drills (Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Write from Dictation — high-yield items), template answers, AI scoring feedback on speaking/writing, full mock tests with score prediction, vocabulary/collocation trainers.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 5

## Cambridge C1 Advanced (CAE) / C2 Proficiency (CPE)
- **Full name + administering body:** Cambridge English Qualifications — C1 Advanced (CAE), C2 Proficiency (CPE); Cambridge University Press & Assessment (UK).
- **Purpose:** Deep-market academic/professional qualifications; accepted by thousands of universities (esp. UK, EU, Canada, Australia — C1 Advanced accepted for Australian student visas) and many employers. CPE signals near-native proficiency.
- **Format:** 4 papers: Reading & Use of English (1h30), Writing (1h30), Listening (~40 min), Speaking (15 min, face-to-face with an examiner pair); total ~4h. Reported on the Cambridge English Scale (C1 pass = 180+; C2 = 200+). Paper or computer at authorized centers.
- **Cost:** No global price — set per center: approx. $150–$300 typical range (UK ~£220–250; US ~$400 at some centers; India/Asia often cheaper). Widest fee variance of any test here.
- **Frequency:** Fixed dates several times per year per level (roughly monthly for C1 in large markets; C2 fewer dates).
- **Score validity:** Certificate does not expire (lifetime) — but most universities and immigration bodies only accept results earned within the last 2–3 years.
- **Popularity:** Not published per level. Cambridge English overall claims multi-million annual test volume across all levels; C1/C2 volume estimated in the low hundreds of thousands/year (approx., unverifiable).
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Grammar/lexical-system teaching (Use of English is unique — open cloze, word formation, key word transformations), essay/report writing for Part 1/2, phrasal verb and idiom banks, listening with transcript analysis, graded reading, full mock exams with grade conversion to the Cambridge English Scale.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 4

## TOEFL Essentials
- **Full name + administering body:** TOEFL Essentials Test; ETS (USA).
- **Status (verified):** Still offered as of 2026 — NOT discontinued. ETS registration pages are live, third-party "2026" guides are being published, and the test was recognized by Canada's IRCC in August 2025. It remains a niche product: accepted by ~500+ programs, and some universities explicitly exclude it — treat it as a secondary option in any content plan.
- **Purpose:** Budget, friendly, at-home sibling of TOEFL iBT for undergraduate/graduate admissions in the US/Canada and beyond.
- **Format:** ~1.5 hours, adaptive, at home only: Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking + unscored personal video statement; overall band 1–12 with section bands.
- **Cost:** ~$100–$120 depending on location (approx.).
- **Frequency:** Regularly scheduled at-home windows, multiple days per week (verify current calendar on ets.org).
- **Score validity:** 2 years.
- **Popularity:** Low/niche — no reliable public annual figures; far behind iBT.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Similar to iBT but adapted to band scoring and short-task pacing; low-cost short course + practice tests; mainly worth bundling into a TOEFL product rather than a standalone site.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 3

## SECTION B — NATIONAL UNIVERSITY ENTRANCE EXAMS

## Gaokao (China)
- **Full name + administering body:** 普通高等学校招生全国统一考试 — National College Entrance Examination; China Ministry of Education + provincial authorities.
- **Purpose:** Sole mainstream gateway to Chinese undergraduate admissions. **Internationally accepted:** growing — most Australian Go8 universities, Cambridge and some UK universities, University of Toronto and other Canadian schools, Italian universities (Marco Polo/Turandot programs, min ~380/750), Hong Kong/Singapore/Macau institutions; commonly combined with an English test. "Gaokao + study abroad" dual-track planning is a rising trend.
- **Format:** Paper-based, 2–4 days (June 7–8/9–10 depending on province). Core: Chinese, Mathematics, Foreign Language (mostly English), + provincial elective model ("3+1+2" reform). Max 750 points in most provinces (Shanghai 660, Hainan 900).
- **Cost:** Varies by province, approx. ¥50–250 (~$7–35) (approx.).
- **Frequency:** Once a year (June).
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle (some foreign universities accept scores from the current or previous year).
- **Popularity:** 13.35M registered (2025); peak 13.42M (2024); slight decline expected 2026–2030 (12.6–13M range forecast).
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Subject teaching at extreme depth (math, physics, chemistry, biology, Chinese, English), provincial syllabus variants, past-paper banks with step-marked solutions, timed paper simulations, score-line databases by university/province, prediction/estimation tools, "Gaokao score → foreign university admission" mapping content.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 3

## JEE Main / JEE Advanced (India)
- **Full name + administering body:** Joint Entrance Examination; National Testing Agency (Main), one of seven IITs rotating (Advanced).
- **Purpose:** Gateway to engineering admission: Main → NITs/IIITs/state colleges + qualifier for Advanced → IITs. NRI/foreign candidates use it via DASA and similar quotas; some overseas universities recognize high JEE scores as STEM-readiness evidence.
- **Format:** Main: computer-based, Physics/Chemistry/Math, 300 marks, reported as percentile; two sessions/year (Jan, Apr). Advanced: two 3-hour papers, once a year (May), variable question formats.
- **Cost:** Main ~₹1,000 (~$12) per session (Gen male; lower for women/reserved categories); Advanced ~₹2,800 (approx.).
- **Frequency:** Main twice/year; Advanced once/year.
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle.
- **Popularity:** Main 2025: ~1.55M unique registrants (~2.45M appearances across two sessions); Advanced: ~200k appeared (approx.).
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Class 11–12 PCM concept teaching (NCERT-based), chapter-wise question banks (30+ years of past papers), problem-solving video solutions, full-length CBT mocks with rank/percentile prediction, revision notes, formula sheets, doubt-solving tools.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 2

## NEET (India)
- **Full name + administering body:** National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (UG); National Testing Agency.
- **Purpose:** Sole gateway to MBBS/BDS in India. Critically, Indian regulation (FMGL 2021) requires NEET qualification for Indian citizens who want to study medicine **abroad** and return to practice — making NEET prep and "NEET + MBBS abroad" a huge combined market. Also taken by NRI/OCI/foreign candidates.
- **Format:** Pen-and-paper OMR, 3 hours, 180 MCQs, Physics/Chemistry/Biology, 720 marks.
- **Cost:** ₹1,700 (~$20) General; ₹9,500 (~$115) for candidates outside India.
- **Frequency:** Once a year (May).
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle (result used for the same year's counseling).
- **Popularity:** 2.27M registered (2025); record 2.33M (2024) — India's largest single exam.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** NCERT-focused PCB teaching, diagram-based biology drills, 10-year PYQ banks, mock OMR tests with All-India-rank simulation, chapter weightage analytics, plus "study MBBS abroad after NEET" guidance content.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 3

## CUET (India)
- **Full name + administering body:** Common University Entrance Test (UG/PG); National Testing Agency.
- **Purpose:** Common admission test for ~250+ central, state, and participating universities in India (DU, BHU, etc.), including many programs popular with NRI/foreign applicants; replaced university-specific merit systems.
- **Format:** Computer-based, candidate chooses up to 5 subjects (languages, domain subjects, general test); each paper 60 min, 50 MCQs (2025 pattern); results normalized.
- **Cost:** ₹1,000 (~$12) for up to 3 subjects + ₹400/extra subject (General).
- **Frequency:** Once a year (May–June).
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle.
- **Popularity:** ~1.35M unique registered (2025), ~1.07M appeared; slight decline vs 2024.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Domain-subject NCERT teaching, general aptitude test (GAT) practice, English/foreign-language section prep, university+program cutoff (previous-year) databases, mock CBTs with normalization explanation.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 2

## CSAT / Suneung (South Korea)
- **Full name + administering body:** College Scholastic Ability Test (대학수학능력시험); Korea Institute for Curriculum and Evaluation (KICE).
- **Purpose:** Gatekeeper for Korean university admissions (locals). International applicants to Korean universities usually go through separate international-admissions tracks (GPA + language tests) and generally do **not** need CSAT; some international tracks accept Gaokao/JEE/other national exams instead.
- **Format:** Paper-based, one day (2nd Thursday of November), 8 periods across ~8 hours: Korean, Mathematics, English, Korean History + Social/Science/Vocational Explore, Second Foreign Language. Standardized scores, percentiles, and 1–9 grade bands per subject.
- **Cost:** approx. ₩30,000–40,000 (~$22–30) (approx.).
- **Frequency:** Once a year (November).
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle.
- **Popularity:** ~554,000 test takers (Nov 2025 exam; approx. — registration vs appearance figures vary); ~31% are repeat takers.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Killer math/Korean problem banks (the exam's difficulty is the product), killer-problem strategy content, grade-1 (1등급) strategy guides, mock exams aligned to KICE style, score-conversion tables, second-language subject coverage.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 2

## EJU (Japan)
- **Full name + administering body:** Examination for Japanese University Admission for International Students (日本留学試験); JASSO (Japan Student Services Organization).
- **Purpose:** The dedicated international-student exam for Japanese universities — used by ~500 universities, including nearly all national universities. By design it is for international applicants (foreign nationals), not locals.
- **Format:** Paper-based; subjects chosen per target university: Japanese as a Foreign Language (reading/listening/writing; 125 min), Science (choose 2 of Physics/Chem/Bio; 80 min), Japan and the World (80 min), Mathematics Course 1 or 2 (80 min). Questions in Japanese or English (per subject choice). Japanese scored 0–400 (+writing 0–50), other subjects 0–200.
- **Cost:** ¥10,000 (~$65) 1 subject / ¥18,000 (~$120) 2+ subjects (domestic, post-2024 revision); overseas fees set locally.
- **Frequency:** Twice a year (June, November); held in Japan + ~11–17 overseas cities.
- **Score validity:** Used per admission cycle (scores kept ~2 years for applications; verify per university).
- **Popularity:** ~30.8k applicants / ~26.9k examinees per session (2026 1st session, JASSO) → ~55–60k applicants/year (approx.).
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Subject teaching in both Japanese and English, JLPT-aligned Japanese support, past EJU papers with solutions, university-specific subject-combination guidance, score-requirement database per university/faculty, mock exams.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 5

## YKS / TYT / AYT (Turkey)
- **Full name + administering body:** Yükseköğretim Kurumları Sınavı, comprising TYT (Basic Proficiency Test) and AYT (Field Proficiency Tests) + YDT (Foreign Language Test); ÖSYM.
- **Purpose:** Sole central gateway for Turkish citizens. **International students do not take YKS** — they apply via the separate international-student quota using YÖS (foreign student exam), SAT, A-Levels, IB, etc. YKS content overlaps heavily with YÖS prep (math/geometry/logic), so it matters indirectly for content production.
- **Format:** TYT: 120 questions, 135 min (Turkish, Math, Social, Science basics); AYT: field sessions 180 min (Sayısal/Sözel/Eşit Ağırlık tracks); YDT: foreign language 80 min. Standardized scoring, placement up to 500+ composite.
- **Cost:** 450 TL (~$10) per session in 2025 → up to ~1,350 TL (~$32) for all three (approx.).
- **Frequency:** Once a year (TYT Saturday + AYT/YDT Sunday, June).
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle.
- **Popularity:** Record 2.56M applicants for 2025-YKS (TYT); ~1.72M took AYT; ~215k YDT.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** TYT/AYT math and Turkish-language drills, topic-by-topic video lessons, net-calculation and point-estimation calculators, ÖSYM past-paper archives (a signature feature), ranking prediction, plus YÖS content for the international segment.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 1 (2 if bundling YÖS content)

## ENT / UNT (Kazakhstan)
- **Full name + administering body:** Единое Национальное Тестирование (Unified National Testing, renamed from UNT in 2024); Ministry of Education / National Testing Center.
- **Purpose:** School-leavers' exam + university admission + state-grant allocation for Kazakhstani students. International applicants to Kazakh universities use a separate international channel (not ENT), so relevance to inbound internationals is minimal — but it is the core product market for Kazakh-speaking users.
- **Format:** Computer-based; two compulsory blocks (Reading Literacy, Mathematical Literacy) + 2 profile subjects chosen from ~10; Kazakh/Russian (rarely English) medium; composite score up to 140 (post-2024 scoring, approx.); two attempts per cycle.
- **Cost:** Free for current-year school graduates; 5,640 KZT (~$11) for repeat/other takers (2024+).
- **Frequency:** Multiple windows per year (January, main May–June wave, additional windows; effectively 2 attempts per graduate).
- **Score validity:** Single admission cycle.
- **Popularity:** ~216k graduates applied in 2025 (~399k test attempts incl. second tries; 342k attempts in 2024) — growing.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** Reading/math literacy drills, profile-subject teaching aligned to the national curriculum, official-format mock ENTs, grant-threshold calculators by score, Kazakh/Russian bilingual delivery.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 1

## ENEM (Brazil)
- **Full name + administering body:** Exame Nacional do Ensino Médio; INEP (Ministry of Education).
- **Purpose:** Brazil's national exam used for public-university admission (SISU), scholarships (ProUni), student finance (FIES), and school-leaving certification. **Internationally:** Portuguese universities accept ENEM scores under a bilateral agreement — the main "ENEM for study abroad" route.
- **Format:** Paper-based, two Sundays in November: Day 1 — Languages, Social Sciences, essay (90 MCQs + 1 essay); Day 2 — Natural Sciences, Mathematics (90 MCQs). IRT (TRI) scoring, 0–1000 per area + essay 0–1000.
- **Cost:** R$85 (~$15) — among the world's cheapest; ~3.05M of 2025 registrants got fee waivers.
- **Frequency:** Once a year (two Sundays in November).
- **Score validity:** Scores reusable for a few years for some programs (Portuguese universities generally accept recent-year scores; verify) — otherwise per-cycle.
- **Popularity:** 4.81M confirmed registrations (2025); 4.33M (2024); 3.93M (2023) — rising.
- **Prep-hub content scope:** All five knowledge-area banks with TRI-aware practice, essay (redação) model texts and structure training with scoring rubrics, full simulations ("simulados"), score-estimation tools, cutoff ("nota de corte") trackers for SISU/ProUni, plus Portugal-application guidance.
- **Relevance for international applicants:** 2 (3 for the Brazil→Portugal corridor specifically)

## Market notes

### 1. Duolingo English Test & PTE acceptance (2025–2026)
- **DET:** 5,500–6,500+ accepting institutions; ~98 of the top 100 US universities; strong in UK (Imperial, Warwick, Southampton, Liverpool), Canada, Australia, Ireland, and expanding fast in China and India. Caveat: **not accepted for UK student visas** (not a SELT) and excluded by some individual programs.
- **PTE Academic:** 3,500+ institutions; near-universal in Australia/NZ; approved for **UK visa** (PTE Academic UKVI is a Home Office SELT); accepted for **Canadian economic immigration** (PTE Core, since 2024) and by most Canadian DLIs; US acceptance now exceeds 3,000 institutions incl. many top schools.
- **Trend:** Both have moved from "alternative" to mainstream at university level; visa/immigration routes remain the moat around IELTS (plus PTE UKVI, LanguageCert, Trinity in the UK; TOEFL iBT was suspended for Australian visas in 2024 — verify current status per route).

### 2. Typical minimum English requirements (popular destinations)
- **US:** IELTS 6.5 (6.0–6.5 UG; 7.0+ for elites) / TOEFL 80–100 (90+ common at top-50; 100+ for top-20) / DET 105–135 / PTE 55–70.
- **UK:** IELTS 6.0–6.5 UG (6.5–7.0 for top unis; band minima 5.5–6.0); student visa requires an approved SELT at the level set by the university.
- **Canada:** IELTS 6.5 (no band <6.0) or TOEFL 86–100 at Toronto/UBC/McGill-tier schools.
- **Australia:** IELTS 6.5 (Go8 standard) or equivalent (PTE 58–65, TOEFL 79–90); student-visa minimums lower.
- **Germany:** IELTS 6.0–6.5 or TOEFL 80–100 for English-taught programs (German-taught need DSH/TestDaF).
- **Netherlands:** IELTS 6.5 (subscores 6.0) or TOEFL iBT 90–92 (e.g., TU/e) for research universities; 6.0 for applied-sciences universities; Cambridge C1 Advanced 176–190 also common.
- Rule of thumb: **IELTS 6.5 overall / TOEFL 90 / DET 120 / PTE 60–65** is the "safe" default minimum at reputable universities in all six destinations.

### 3. Growth and demand signals
- **Fastest growing: Duolingo English Test** — bookings grew ~40%+ YoY (2024), 3M+ cumulative test takers (2025), acceptance list growing faster than any other test; low price ($65) + unlimited score sends is the structural advantage.
- **PTE Academic** — the fastest large-scale challenger to IELTS: 822k (2022) → 1.23M tests (2023, +50%); strong 2024–2025 growth driven by UK/Australia/Canada visa acceptance.
- **IELTS** still the volume leader (>4M tests, 2023) and the only test at scale in both admissions and global migration; TOEFL has stagnated (~1.7M/yr est.) and lost the Australian visa channel in 2024.
- **Newer digital entrants:** Oxford ELLT surpassed 100k tests (2024); LanguageCert expanding test-centre networks. AI-powered assessment market: $1.88B (2025) → forecast $10.6B (2034), ~21% CAGR.
- **Product implication:** a prep platform should treat DET + PTE as growth bets, IELTS as the anchor (largest TAM, best SEO demand), and bundle TOEFL content for the US/Canada segment.
